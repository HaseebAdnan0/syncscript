- Learnings for future iterations:
  - Vault deletion triggers post_delete signals on VaultMembership that try to create audit logs
  - AuditLog has FK to Vault with CASCADE, but signals fire AFTER vault is deleted
  - Solution: Temporarily disconnect post_delete signal during vault deletion using try/finally
  - Pattern: `post_delete.disconnect(handler, sender=Model)` before delete, reconnect in finally
  - Test cascade deletion by capturing IDs before delete, verifying they don't exist after
  - VaultSerializer context={'request': request} needed for user_role field computation
